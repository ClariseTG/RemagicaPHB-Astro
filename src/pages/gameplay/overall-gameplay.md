# Introduction

Remagica is a table-top roleplaying game played with 4-7 people; 1 is the GM, and the other 3-5 are players.

The goal of the game is to tell a story as a group. Everyone makes a character to play, and the GM either makes or runs the setting.

Mainly, all parties will be role-playing, but the GM will have other focuses as well, such as describing the world and making encounters.

## Getting Started: Players

If you're a player, you should start by <a href="/gameplay/checks">reading the rules on checks</a> and <a href="/characters/tutorial">making a character</a>!
