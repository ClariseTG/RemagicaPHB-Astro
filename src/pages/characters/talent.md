# Talents

A character's Talent is the parts of themself they rely on. These are the skills they brought with them from their life before adventuring; they will have a profound effect on the Skills they will learn as they develop.

A Talent gives a character one Talent Skill (often abbreviated to "Skill"). Skills give characters new options or make them better at things they can already do. They also unlock other Skills that the character can take later on, as seen in the [Skills List](/compendium/talent-skills).

The Talents a character can begin with are:

## Agile
The most agile have learned to rely on their quick reactions and sharp instincts. They can act before they can think; often, this can save their life when trouble comes to get them. Wired and unpredictable, those whose talents lie in their speed and reflexes are difficult to pin down.

## Astute
Exceptionally gifted with a variety of skills, those who are astute know a lot about the world around them, and what they can do in it. Whether they are a researcher or a jack of all trades, it is their varied abilities that define them.

## Charming
Some connect with the world around them through other people. The most charming often have many friends-- often in strange places-- and connections they can rely on. Whether their skill is making new friends, knowing the right people, or pretending to form deeper connections, those who are talented with their charms can often find unique ways of navigating any situation.

## Creative
Creative types see beauty in the things around them, and carry with them the ability to enhance the qualities they feel. Through craft or performance, those with creative talent can work with the things around them to make them into something new.

## Faithful
The world is filled with forces beyond comprehension, some sapient. Those with strong faith have dedicated their time to learning about those forces and working alongside them for a purpose. While some choose their own purpose, others find themselves corrupted by the forces around them and are compelled to act alongside their force's will.

## Keen
Attentive and curious, keener folk keep a sharp eye out for anything of interest. They're well attuned to their environment, knowing where things are and what to do with them. They understand purpose where others do not.

## Tough
The toughest of us can rely on their physical might to keep themselves going and control the situation around them. There is technique to asserting strength, developed over years of use-- those who are tough know how to use themselves to the fullest.
