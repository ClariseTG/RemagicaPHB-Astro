# Magic Archetype

A certain malleability of the soul gives each faun a unique magic ability. These abilities are shaped by the soul, formed in tandem with the thoughts and way of being of their user. Magic may be an in-born ability, but magic is not often available from birth. Instead, it emerges as a creature ages and is realized as a person.

Just as there are infinite configurations of the soul, there are infinite configurations of magic. However, some patterns are far more common than others, and magic abilities were categorized into Archetypes describing their usage.

When creating a character, know that their magic ability can come in any shape, but often a very specific one. Linoda Phoenix solidifies fire and sculpts it with her hands, and Saint Ionas of the Blindsight Curch manipulates the direction and strength of gravity in a small area. A character you play should have a similarly specific ability.

These Archetypes serve as a way for characters to quickly know how others use magic, for teamwork, tutelage, or community. Mechanically, they define how a magic ability interacts with the other mechanics of the system. Choose one that best fits your character and their soul.
