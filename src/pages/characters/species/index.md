# Species

Sapience is not purely for those that walk on two legs in Urkaya. There are many different species of faun in this world, with many body shapes and ways of being.

The word *faun* (['fɐ.ʊn] or "fah-uhn") refers to anything that is, unambiguously, a person. This includes all of the species that are playable, and distinguishes still-sapient things like non-lucid gods-- non-faun-- from the kinds of creature you might be. Faunhood is a contentious topic in some places, and there may be entities you consider to be a person that a character from Urkaya may not consider faun.

All faun share a certain malleability of the soul, which is what enables them to each have [unique magical abilities](/characters/magic), and almost always co-occurs with sapience and higher thought. It is theorized that one causes the other.
