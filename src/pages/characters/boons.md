# Boons and Traits

Many people walk, swim, and climb the lands and waters of Urkaya, and each forges their path in their own way. Some are touched by gods and otherworldly things; others are born different; and still others' bodies change as their life progresses.

Boons and Traits are structurally similar to Talent Skills, but they represent unique facets of a character's life, rather than a progression of knowledge. Boons are strictly positive, and only one can be chosen for a character as they're created; Traits are only *different*, and as many can be chosen as is needed, and more can be added as the story calls for them.

One of the goals for Traits is to include disabilities. The list of Traits, like the lists of Skills and Tags, should grow! If you think a Trait should be added, create an issue for it [on the github](). Github issues should also be used for Traits that are written badly or that aren't reflective of their corresponding reality.
