# Statistics, Proficiencies, and Checks

A Statistic (or Stat) is a number value representing some of the broad traits of a character. Stats are integers from -7 to 8; a higher number means that the character is stronger in that suit, and a lower number means that they are worse with it.

A Proficiency is a number value representing a character's skill in something more specific. They are whole numbers starting at 0. A higher number means that the character is more proficient at the set of tasks the proficiency represents.

## Rolling Checks

When a character attempts to do something, and the outcome is uncertain, they make a [Check](/gameplay/checks).

## Statistics

- **Body**: The character's physicality. A character that can tough out poisons, lift large weights, and endure through hard labor would have a high Body stat. Characters with low Body might have weak immune systems or have difficulty with some strength-related tasks.
- **Will**: The character's strength of mind. A character with high Will can endure pain, keep magic under control, and stand strong in the face of terror. A character without Will may be quick to snap under pressure or lose themselves to the torrent of the unknown.
- **Senses**: Not only the character's literal senses, but also the character's ability to make connections and notice details. A character that notices details, detects untruths, and infers things about others has strong Senses. A character with poor Senses could be someone who struggles to pick out things in their environment or someone who is dense.
- **Charisma**: The character's weight in the minds of others. A character with a lot of Charisma is not necessarily good-looking, but quickly gains and retains the respect or adoration of others. Their words carry sway. Characters without Charisma may be spoken over and overlooked for their talents.

## Proficiencies

When a character makes a check, they add the relevant Proficiency to the roll. There are three types of proficiencies in Remagica: Skill Proficiencies, Tool Proficiencies, and Lore Proficiencies. All three types are functionally quite similar; however, the three types are segregated in character creation and can be segregated during advancements.

### Tool Proficiencies

Tool Proficiencies represent how proficient a character is with a tool, which is an item with the Tool Object Tag. All weapons are tools, and there are other items that are tools, such as forgery kits or carving tools.

A character can have a Tool Proficiency in any single tool that exists. Some tools can share a Tool Proficiency; for example, a Warhammer and a Moonstone Warhammer would share a proficiency. If a Tool shares its proficiency with another, it will say so in its item description.

### Lore Proficiencies

Lore Proficiencies represent categories of knowledge the character has. This list is not enumerated, and the GM and player should agree on them. They should be broad or applicable enough to come up in play.

For example, these are appropriate Lore Proficiencies:

- Engineering
- Philosophy and Literature
- Biology

A character can (and is encouraged to) have a more specific interest or area of deep study if they are an academic, but those methods can apply more broadly, hence the broader categories.
