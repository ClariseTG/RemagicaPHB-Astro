# Statistics, Proficiencies, and Checks

A Statistic is a number value representing some of the broad traits of a character. Statistics are integers from -7 to 8; a higher number means that the character is stronger in that suit, and a lower number means that they are worse with it.

A Proficiency is a number value representing a character's skill in something more specific. They are whole numbers starting at 0. A higher number means that the character is more proficient at the set of tasks the proficiency represents.

## Rolling Checks

When a character attempts to do something, and the outcome is uncertain, they make a [Check]().
